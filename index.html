About International Beauty Skills Academy

Welcome to International Beauty Skills Academy — your destination for becoming a certified expert in the beauty industry. We offer industry-standard courses in Hair , Makeup , Skin , and Nail Art , designed to help you master both foundational and advanced techniques.

Our programs are led by seasoned professionals with years of salon and teaching experience. Whether you're just starting out or looking to upgrade your skills, we provide hands-on training in state-of-the-art classrooms that simulate real salon environments.

We don’t just teach — we guide you toward a successful career. We offer job placement support, client-handling techniques, and even entrepreneurial training for those who want to open their own salon.

At International Beauty Skills Academy, our mission is to build confidence, creativity, and professionalism in every student. Join hundreds of successful graduates who are now thriving in the beauty and wellness world.
